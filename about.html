About Claire

I stand beside your leaders, steady the situation, and move the team forward with measured, practical purpose.

Claire Poole is a Marketing, Communications and Brand Executive with senior leadership experience across North America and Europe. Founder & CEO of Clairify Communications, she steps in, as permanent leader, fractional executive, or turnaround specialist, when organizations need a problem fixed, a leadership gap bridged, or their people developed.

Clear Results

I've built and reset communications functions for global organizations, led teams through crisis, guided publicly traded companies through rebranding, and given executives the clarity and confidence to lead through the hardest moments.

I founded Clairify Communications in 2005 and have since built a practice trusted by organizations across nine industry sectors.

Before that, I led communications inside some of Canada's most complex organizations: directing a bilingual team across six national markets at one of the world's largest law firms, repositioning a publicly traded manufacturing company as its Director of Brand and Communications, and contributing to Indigenous relations, HR, and foundation communications at one of Canada's largest energy companies.

Rapid onboarding. Fast integration into the business. Steady leadership from the start. That's what clients hire Clairify for, built on experience agency-side and in-house, across crisis and calm, sector after sector.
