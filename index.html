Corporate structures are changing. Fewer organisations need a full time communications, marketing, or brand executive on staff, but every organisation still needs senior counsel to protect and develop engagement, brand, and reputation. Communications Concierge Services meets the business where it's at, providing that executive level judgment on a monthly retainer, scaled to what the organisation needs right now, with no long term commitment and none of the cost of a full time hire.

Outcomes
  • Executive Presence. Senior marketing, communications, and brand leadership represented internally and externally, without adding a full time executive to headcount.
  • Brand Consistency. One consistent voice across marketing campaigns, communications, and every public facing decision.
  • Continuity. An embedded advisor who already knows the organisation's priorities and audiences, and can respond immediately rather than starting from scratch.
  • Cost Efficiency. Executive level counsel at a small fraction of the salary, benefits, and overhead of a full time hire.
  • Flexibility. Support that meets the business where it's at, scaled up or down as needs change, with no long term contract.
Scope of Services
  • Brand & Marketing Strategy. Positioning, messaging, and campaign review that build brand preference and keep every market consistent.
  • Corporate Communications. Ongoing counsel that keeps leadership, employees, and external audiences aligned as the organisation changes.
  • Issue Management. A plan in place before a risk becomes a crisis, and senior counsel the moment one surfaces.
  • Content Review and Governance. Senior review of key content and materials before they go public, so nothing publishes without an executive level check.
  • Monthly Strategy Session. A standing call to review priorities, upcoming initiatives, and communications needs.
  • Ongoing Access. Senior counsel by phone or email within retainer hours for time sensitive decisions.
