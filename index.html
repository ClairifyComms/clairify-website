A one-on-one engagement, delivered directly by CLAIRIFY to a high-potential professional, that teaches hesitant or passive employees how to establish their professional gravitas and leadership style.

Outcomes
  • Leadership Capacity. A successor-track professional equipped with the ownership, presence, and language to operate at the next level.

Investment
